## Featured Projects

### LPO Extraction Platform
Multimodal document extraction platform that processes 1,500 Local Purchase Orders in 10 minutes at 98% accuracy, with only 2% routed to manual review. Benchmarked Amazon Bedrock Nova 2 Lite and OpenAI GPT-5 before migrating to Anthropic Claude Haiku and Sonnet for higher accuracy on scanned and image-based POs, with a prompt-engineered chain of system and follow-up prompts and master data for entity matching and normalization.

`Next.js` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Anthropic Claude` `Docker`

---

### Agentic Content Automation
Autonomous multi-agent pipeline: a scraper agent ingests and LLM-scores 50+ RSS and news feeds daily, a content generation agent writes brand-specific LinkedIn and Instagram captions and image prompts, and a feedback guardrail agent approves output before webhook-triggered n8n workflows publish cross-platform. Brand posts have exceeded 1,000 views.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `OpenAI` `n8n` `AWS S3`

---

### Property Investment Intelligence
MSc dissertation project that predicts and explains the gross rental yield of Dubai properties and turns a pasted Bayut or Dubizzle listing into an invest-or-avoid verdict. Trained on 117k Dubai Land Department sales and 250k Ejari rent contracts, with XGBoost selected over LightGBM and Random Forest, SHAP explanations for every prediction, and an LLM-generated narrative.

`Python` `FastAPI` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `LangChain` `Next.js` `Supabase`

[GitHub](https://github.com/AndrewRaj0510/Property_Investment_Intelligence)

---

### Microsoft CloudOptima
Team-built multi-agent cloud architecture designer: describe your infrastructure, and five AI agents (Architect, Cost Analyst, Security Engineer, Compliance Officer, and Judge) collaborate to design, cost, secure, and validate an Azure deployment. In development.

`Python` `Pydantic` `Azure OpenAI` `Nvidia NIM` `Streamlit`

[GitHub](https://github.com/G-Narendra/Microsoft_CloudOptima)

---

### Job Scraper Console
Browser-based console that scrapes job postings and client leads from Reddit, LinkedIn, and Upwork on user-defined parameters, with rate-limited endpoints and a persisted run history. Reduced manual effort for the HR and marketing teams.

`Next.js` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `AWS S3` `Apify`

---

### Hybrid RAG Assistant
Retrieval-augmented generation system that answers natural language questions on HR, finance, payroll, and performance data by routing queries across PostgreSQL, pgvector, and a knowledge graph. Prompt caching and token optimization cut inference cost.

`Python` `LangChain` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `Knowledge Graph`

---

### Stock Predictor
Browser-based stock prediction platform tracking 60+ stocks. Ingests daily market data, engineers technical features, and runs Random Forest, XGBoost, and Logistic Regression models with SHAP explainability on a live dashboard.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Python` `FastAPI` `DuckDB`

[GitHub](https://github.com/AndrewRaj0510/Stock_Predictor)
